FULL DESCRIPTION (paste into DevPost):

Subnet Tutor is an offline, on-device educational application built for the Africa Deep Tech Challenge 2026 (ADTC). It teaches IP subnetting through Keith Barker's finger-counting method, combining math reasoning with hands-on practice — fully bilingual in English and Arabic.

WHAT IT DOES
• Interactive tutorial: raise fingers to count powers of 2 (2, 4, 8…256 subnets)
• 4-level subnetting game with deterministic grading
• On-device AI tutor: Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (GGUF Q4_K_M) via llama.cpp + local RAG
• 100% offline after one-time model download

ADTC ALIGNMENT
• Domain: Math & Scientific Reasoning
• Runtime: llama.cpp (mandatory GGUF)
• Peak RSS: 1655 MB | TPS: 7.36 tok/s (profiler run)
• African use case: offline IT education for schools without reliable internet
